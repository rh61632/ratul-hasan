Audit Scope & Industrial Context

Asian Marble Industries Bangladesh Ltd. is a prominent stone manufacturing and slab processing enterprise headquartered in Jashore. The processing facility transforms raw natural stone and imported marble blocks into precision-finished tiles, kitchen tops, and dimensioned architectural slabs through heavy mechanical sawing, water-lubricated milling, and multistage polishing.

Given the continuous operation of high-power three-phase induction motors (ranging from 15 kW up to 110 kW), continuous pumping loads for slurry water recycling, and pneumatic actuation systems, energy expenditures constitute a major portion of operational costs. The 4-member engineering audit team was tasked with performing a comprehensive facility walkthrough, documenting electrical balance, examining Power Factor Improvement (PFI) mechanisms, and identifying specific avenues to reduce kW consumption and optimize plant utility.

Core Audit Objective: "Identify electrical power factor deficiencies, quantify motor running conditions and transmission belt slippage, evaluate water recirculation pump efficiencies, and recommend actionable energy management protocols that enhance equipment longevity while delivering measurable monthly electricity savings."

Key Technical Audit Focus Areas

The engineering audit assessed the facility across four core operational subsystems:

Power Factor & Capacitor Banks

Inspected the low-voltage Power Factor Improvement (PFI) panel, assessing contactors, capacitor cell integrity, and automatic switching relays. Proper reactive power compensation prevents utility penalties and reduces I²R distribution line thermal losses.

Heavy Motor Drives & Gang Saws

Examined 110 kW (150 HP, 192 A) induction motors driving multi-blade diamond gang saws and block cutters. Logged rated vs. operational RPM using a digital non-contact tachometer to verify slip factors and belt tension integrity.

Closed-Loop Slurry Water Recycling

Surveyed the multi-stage sedimentation ponds and centrifugal pump station. Water is continuously separated from stone slurry and recirculated to blade cooling nozzles, conserving fresh groundwater while optimizing pumping head.

Pneumatic Compressors & Auxiliaries

Inspected belt-driven reciprocating air compressor receiver tanks, pressure regulator gauges, and distribution airlines used for pneumatic stone clamping and gantry feed controls.

Data-Backed Recommendations & Findings

Based on the on-site measurements and operational observations, the audit formulated the following key engineering recommendations:

  • Capacitor Bank Routine Servicing: Periodically measure individual capacitance values in the PFI cabinet to detect degraded capacitors and maintain an active plant power factor above 0.95, avoiding tariff surcharges.
  • Drive Belt Tensioning & Alignment: Maintain systematic tensioning schedules for the multi-V-belt sheaves on high-power gang saw motors to reduce transmission slip losses and bearing wear.
  • Water Recirculation Pump Scheduling: Coordinate pump operating duty with sawing station operational schedules to eliminate unloaded running and reduce hydraulic friction head.
  • Compressed Air Leak Auditing: Implement periodic leak detection routines across pneumatic fittings and quick-release valves on bridge cutting and polishing stations to minimize compressor cycling frequency.
