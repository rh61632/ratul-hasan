About the Competition

The IEEE Bangladesh Section SYW Idea Pitching Competition 2025 was held as part of the Vitality Enhancement Summit 2025 — the flagship annual summit of the IEEE Bangladesh Section organized for Student members, Young Professionals, and Women in Engineering. The event took place on 29th August, 2025 at Asia Hotel & Resorts, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Teams were challenged to identify a real-world problem, develop a compelling solution concept, and pitch it before an expert evaluation panel under tight time constraints — followed by a rigorous Q&A round where panel members probed the feasibility and depth of each proposal.

Team Dynamite

The winning team was a cross-functional, cross-university group of 5 students — representing 5 different universities from across Bangladesh, brought together under the banner of Team Dynamite.
