About the Competition

The IEEE Bangladesh Section SYW Idea Pitching Competition 2025 was held as part of the Vitality Enhancement Summit 2025 — the flagship annual summit of the IEEE Bangladesh Section organized for Student members, Young Professionals, and Women in Engineering. The event took place on 29th August, 2025 at Asia Hotel & Resorts, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Teams were challenged to identify a real-world problem, develop a compelling solution concept, and pitch it before an expert evaluation panel under tight time constraints — followed by a rigorous Q&A round where panel members probed the feasibility and depth of each proposal.

Team Dynamite

The winning team was a cross-functional, cross-university group of 5 students — representing 5 different universities from across Bangladesh, brought together under the banner of Team Dynamite.

The Winning Pitch: Wearable Maternal Care Screening

Team Dynamite's championship pitch centered on an innovative healthcare solution: "Quick health risk detection during pregnancy using kit built in jewelry". The concept addressed critical barriers to maternal prenatal care in developing regions—where clinic visits are infrequent and early symptoms of preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, and vital instability often go undetected.

By embedding low-power biosensing elements and micro-optical diagnostics discreetly into everyday wearable jewelry, the device enables continuous, stigma-free screening with direct telemetry alerts. The proposal withstood intensive cross-examination by the IEEE evaluator panel regarding sensor feasibility, calibration, power budget, and user ergonomics.

Summit Honor: 2nd Place in National Quiz

In addition to the team's Champion trophy in the idea pitching segment, Md. Ratul Hasan also secured a podium finish in the summit's high-stakes National Summit Quiz (conducted on Kahoot among participants from across all represented university student branches). Scoring 9,091 points to take 2nd place nationwide, Ratul was called to the stage to receive a personalized Certificate of Achievement presented directly by Prof. Saifur Rahman (2023 IEEE Global President & CEO).

Recognition & Award Handover

The team was formally awarded the Certificate of Achievement, handed over in person on the grand stage by Chief Guest Prof. Saifur Rahman (2023 IEEE Global President & CEO) alongside Prof. M. Imamul Hassan Bhuiyan (Chair, IEEE Bangladesh Section), honoring Team Dynamite as Champions of the SYW Idea Pitching Competition 2025.
