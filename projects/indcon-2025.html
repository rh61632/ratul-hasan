About the Competition

The International Industrial Activities Congress (INDCon 2025) is organized under the IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) Bangladesh Chapter. The Presentation Competition challenges university teams to present technical or research work in a structured format before a distinguished evaluation panel comprising senior IEEE faculty and chapter leaders.

As the designated presenter for the 3-member JUST team, the full presentation was delivered solo on stage — covering the technical content, handling all panel interaction, and representing the team through the entire evaluation process.
