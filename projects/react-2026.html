Academic Segment

National Poster Presentation

The Poster Presentation segment of REACT 2026 provided university researchers and young innovators a nationwide academic platform to present technical methodologies, scientific research, and engineering solutions in a formal conference setting.

Participants were required to structure their findings across a technical research poster, present their work to attending delegates and academics, and defend their hypotheses and methodology under a rigorous evaluation by an expert judging panel assessing analytical rigor, design feasibility, and oral defense during the Q&A session.

Detailed research documentation and findings will be updated here following publication.
National Festival Profile

About REACT 2026

REACT 2026 (Research • Engineering • Automation • Computer • Technology) was a two-day premier national festival on science, technology, robotics, and academic innovation organized by the IEEE Southeast University Student Branch and Southeast University, held on 10–11 September 2026 at the Southeast University Permanent Campus, Dhaka.

Powered by Suzuki Motorbikes as the title sponsor, with co-sponsorship from the IEEE Bangladesh Section and collaboration with the IEEE WIE Affinity Group, the festival brought together young researchers, engineers, and competitive roboticists from universities across Bangladesh across six flagship segments:

Official Protocol

Closing & Prize-Giving Ceremony

The grand closing and prize-giving ceremony was held on Friday, 11 September 2026, at 3:00 PM at the Multipurpose Hall of Southeast University’s permanent campus in Dhaka, attended by national dignitaries, academic leaders, and delegations from across the country.

Chief Guest: Mr. Habibur Rashid Habib, MP
Hon’ble State Minister for Railways and Road Transport and Highways Division, Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh
"Technological excellence is indispensable for building a prosperous Bangladesh. All young innovators and winners must continue working tirelessly to contribute to the domestic advancement of science and engineering in the country."
Special Guest: Mr. Rezaul Karim
Hon’ble Chairman of the Board of Trustees, Southeast University
Referring to the participants as "young scientists," he encouraged them to sustain their research efforts toward the technological elevation of the nation, thanking IEEE SEU Student Branch for organizing this transformative event.

The welcome address was delivered by Professor Dr. M. Mofazzal Hossain, Pro-Vice Chancellor of Southeast University, followed by concluding remarks from Professor Dr. Yusuf Mahbubul Islam, Vice Chancellor of Southeast University.
