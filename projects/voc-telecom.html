Telecom Voice-of-Customer (VoC) Intelligence Engine

An enterprise end-to-end NLP and Business Intelligence pipeline analyzing customer sentiment, operational complaints, and product trajectories across Bangladesh’s premier telecommunications operators: Grameenphone Ltd. (operating the MyGP platform), Robi Axiata Limited (operating the My Robi platform), and Banglalink Digital Communications Limited (operating the MyBL platform).

Methodological Equity & Sampling Standards:

1. 3,716-Day Common Duration Window (July 28, 2016 – September 30, 2026 | N = 655,670): To eliminate temporal selection bias and guarantee strict statistical equity (ensuring no operator has missing historical windows in comparative analytics), all published benchmarks, comparative scorecards, trendlines, and category distributions strictly evaluate the continuous 10-year period where all three operators coexist simultaneously. The full multi-year corpus (N = 661,160, Oct 2014 – Oct 2026) is preserved in storage for extended longitudinal modeling.

2. Central Limit Theorem (CLT) Normality Rule (N ≥ 30): In longitudinal time-series analytics, we enforce the classical Central Limit Theorem standard (N ≥ 30) for asymptotic sample normality. Sparse early-adoption months (N < 30, such as July 2016 where Grameenphone had 3 reviews and Robi had 0) are excluded from monthly trendlines to eliminate small-sample distortion, where a single random review would artificially swing net sentiment by ±30% to ±100%.

// Root-Cause Diagnostics

Significant Shock Detection & Additive Category Attribution

To transition from passive metric monitoring to root-cause diagnosis, our pipeline deploys an automated Significant Shock Attribution Engine:

1. Statistical Shock Threshold (Δ NSS ≥ 10 pts)

Across 81 high-volume months, median monthly shift is 2.57 – 2.84 points. A ±10-point swing corresponds to the 90th–95th percentile, isolating high-impact, actionable operational anomalies occurring in <10% of months.

2. Additive Category Decomposition Formula

Because Net Sentiment Score is linearly additive across categories, the month-over-month shift decomposes additively without approximation: Δ NSS = ∑ Δ Contribution_c = ∑ [ (Pos_c - Neg_c) / N_Total ] × 100

Operator Strategic Roadmaps & Competitive Levers

Grameenphone (MyGP)

Scale Anchor
Common Period NSS: +64.9% (320,143 reviews)
2026 Snapshot: +56.8%
Core Vulnerability: Billing transparency (1.11%) and data pack pricing complaints (5.27%).

Strategic Lever: Deploy dynamic micro-packs with rollover validity and a zero-click "Where Did My Balance Go?" transaction timeline to curb airtime deduction grievances.

Robi (My Robi)

NSS Leader
Common Period NSS: +65.9% (198,378 reviews)
2026 Snapshot: +76.2%
Core Vulnerability: Offers & data pack friction (5.18%) and app login session bugs (4.60%).

Strategic Lever: Overhaul biometric login token caching to resolve recurring session timeout crashes, and simplify automated VAS cancellation toggles.

Banglalink (MyBL)

Agile Value Leader
Common Period NSS: +53.1% (137,149 reviews)
2026 Snapshot: +81.9% 🏆
Core Vulnerability: Indoor/suburban 4G latency (3.64%) and technical login glitches (8.12%).

Strategic Lever: Capitalize on market-leading +81.9% sentiment to transition promotional micro-rechargers into recurring monthly commitments while optimizing 4G edge caching.
