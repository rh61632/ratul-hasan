The Story Behind the Data

Over 180 million subscribers voice daily frustrations across app stores, yet their feedback remained buried in noisy Banglish and deceptively simple star ratings. We assumed standard AI could easily parse bilingual slang, and that negative reviews simply tracked network coverage drops. The reality was startling: abrupt app UI redesigns and unexpected tariff shifts triggered far more user churn than poor signal ever did. By fine-tuning a BanglaBERT intelligence engine across 655,000+ customer reviews, we transformed raw rants into instant root-cause attribution—empowering telecom leaders to diagnose and fix breaking customer pain points within hours instead of quarters.

The Story Behind the Data

Over 180 million subscribers voice daily frustrations across app stores, yet their feedback remained buried in noisy colloquial remarks and deceptively simple star ratings. We assumed standard commercial tools could easily parse bilingual slang, and that negative reviews simply tracked network coverage drops. Methodological Equity & Sampling Standards:

1. 3,713-Day Common Duration Window (August 1, 2016 – September 30, 2026 | N = 655,649): To eliminate temporal selection bias and guarantee strict statistical equity (ensuring no operator has missing historical windows in comparative analytics), all published benchmarks, comparative scorecards, trendlines, and category distributions strictly evaluate the continuous 10-year period where all three operators coexist simultaneously. The full multi-year corpus (N = 661,160, Oct 2014 – Oct 2026) is preserved in storage for extended longitudinal modeling.

2. Central Limit Theorem (CLT) Normality Rule (N ≥ 30): In longitudinal time-series analytics, we enforce the classical Central Limit Theorem standard (N ≥ 30) for asymptotic sample normality. Sparse early-adoption months (N < 30, such as July 2016 where Grameenphone had 3 reviews and Robi had 0) are excluded from monthly trendlines to eliminate small-sample distortion, where a single random review would artificially swing net sentiment by ±30% to ±100%.

// Deep Engineering Specification

Production Machine Learning Pipeline & Data Architecture Blueprint

End-to-end technical specification of data pipelines, weak supervision formulations, n-gram vectorizers, calibrated soft-voting ensembles, neural transformers, and linguistic routing modules.

01

Parallel Ingestion & Primary-Key Deduplication

Multi-threaded review ingestion (scraped_data_global/pipeline/run_parallel_scrapers.py) harvests raw Play Store streams with canonical metadata (review_id, app_version, thumbs_up, timestamp). Strict primary-key deduplication on canonical review_id compresses the 661,160 master raw reviews into the continuous 3,713-day common window (655,649 reviews, Aug 1, 2016 – Sep 30, 2026).

02

Weak Supervision & Groq Seed Formulation

Groq cloud inference (qwen/qwen3.8-27b) provides high-throughput weak supervision for 4,500 balanced multi-operator seed records across Grameenphone, Robi, and Banglalink. Weak pseudo-labels are validated through an independent double-blind human ground-truth protocol achieving substantial inter-rater reliability (κ = 0.77 Cohen's Kappa).

03

Dual Character N-Gram Feature Engineering

Sub-word representation uses TfidfVectorizer(analyzer="char_wb", ngram_range=(2, 5), min_df=3, sublinear_tf=True). Slicing sub-word character n-grams across word boundaries directly captures invariant phonetic stems across wildly inconsistent Banglish transliterations (faltu, faaltu, faltoo, baje, vaalo) while mapping native Bengali Unicode (U+0980–U+09FF) and English into one unified sparse feature space.

04

Calibrated Soft-Voting Ensemble [2, 2, 1]

Production classification deploys VotingClassifier(voting="soft", weights=[2, 2, 1]) uniting: (1) Multinomial Logistic Regression (weight=2) for regularized posterior bounds on minority neutral classes; (2) Platt-calibrated LinearSVC (weight=2) for maximum margin hyperplane separation; and (3) ComplementNB (weight=1) acting as a Bayesian prior regularizer that tames overconfident tail probabilities (84.76% down to 74.47%). Benchmarked against statistical baseline floor DummyClassifier(strategy="most_frequent") under StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42).

05

BanglaBERT & Deep Representation Learning

End-to-end backpropagation through BUET BanglaBERT (csebuetnlp/banglabert, 110M params, 12 layers) establishes the state-of-the-art benchmark: 91.60% Accuracy / 0.8598 Macro-F1 on Sentiment and 85.27% Accuracy on Operational Categories (+10.39% over frozen feature baseline). Multilingual MiniLM (384-dim dense embeddings) and PyTorch BiLSTM + Bahdanau Attention serve as neural comparison baselines.

06

Linguistic Cohort Router & Telemetry

Rule-based language classifier (ml/language_detector.py) integrates exact verified thresholds: Bengali script threshold ≥ 25% (script_threshold = 0.25, Unicode U+0980–U+09FF) and English lexical dominance threshold ≥ 70% (dominance_threshold = 0.70) evaluated against nltk.corpus.words, telecom domain vocabulary, and 80+ Romanized Banglish phonetic markers with code-mixed telemetry.

Econometric Decoupling // Reviewer-Mix Isolation

Review Volume Surge Diagnostics & Surge-Filtered Volatility

Audit Script: scripts/analyze_surges.py

Standard standard-deviation and raw RMSSD volatility treat all month-over-month shifts as genuine changes in operational quality. However, empirical analysis of review arrival dynamics reveals that 45.7% of all significant shocks (16 of 35) occur during acute review volume surges (≥ 50% month-over-month volume change), compared to only 13.3% across non-shock transitions.

When review volumes surge (e.g., following marketing campaigns, forced update prompts, or viral social media complaints), the influx is dominated by casual raters who leave short praise or general greetings ("good", "nice", "ok"). Consequently, month-over-month shifts in the General Appreciation / Other category track review volume shifts with an R2 = 0.5601 across shocks. Filtering out volume-surge transitions decouples true operational volatility from reviewer-mix fluctuations:

Hypothesis Testing // Categorical Statistical Verification

Functional Domain Dominance: Software Friction vs. Network Quality

Audit Script: scripts/audit_point1.py

To test whether client-side software instability is the primary driver of customer sentiment disruption, we analyze functional operational category leadership across both shock and non-shock regimes:

Shock Regimes (N = 35 Shocks)

App Login & Technical Bugs leads among functional operational domains in 27 of 35 shocks (77.1%). This effect is especially pronounced in Banglalink (leading 20 of 22 shocks), while Grameenphone (3 of 6) and Robi (4 of 7) exhibit a balance between login friction and tariff/data pack dissatisfaction.

Non-Shock Regimes (N = 205 Transitions)

In baseline operational months, App Login leads functional complaints in 91 of 205 transitions (44.4%) across the market (Grameenphone: 30/74 [40.5%], Robi: 32/73 [43.8%], Banglalink: 29/58 [50.0%]).

Statistical Contingency Tests (2×2 Independence):
  • Pooled Test Across All Transitions: Comparing shock vs. non-shock login leadership yields χ²(1) = 12.83 (p = 0.0003), with a Fisher's Exact Odds Ratio of 4.23 (p = 0.0004), confirming that login friction is disproportionately concentrated during acute sentiment crises.
  • Non-Surge Robustness Test: When excluding volume surges (≥ 50% MoM volume change), the association remains highly robust: χ²(1) = 6.38 (p = 0.0116), Fisher's Exact Odds Ratio = 3.65 (p = 0.0150).

ComplementNB Bayesian Prior Regularization

Calibration Engine

In discrete classification tests, Logistic Regression and Platt-calibrated LinearSVC exhibit overconfident maximum predicted probabilities in high-dimensional TF-IDF space (mean maximum confidence: 84.76%).

Bayesian Prior Smoothing Impact:

Incorporating ComplementNB (norm=True) computes parameter weights from the complement of each category, acting as an empirical entropy regularizer that tames mean maximum confidence from 84.76% down to 74.47%. This eliminates overconfident probability spikes while maintaining full sub-millisecond CPU execution throughput (>80× faster than deep neural networks).

Linguistic Routing // Pre-Processing Engine

Automated Rule-Based Language & Code-Mixing Detector

Module: ml/language_detector.py

Customer reviews in the Bangladesh telecommunications ecosystem arrive in multiple scripts and transliterated dialects. To provide real-time language attribution and route inputs cleanly, our production pipeline integrates a standalone rule-based detector (LanguageClassifier in ml/predict.py):

Stage 1: Bengali Unicode Script Verification

Scans incoming text against the official Bengali Unicode script range (U+0980 to U+09FF). If Bengali characters comprise ≥ 20% of all alphabetic characters, the text is routed as Bangla (Native Script).

Stage 2: Lexical Banglish & English Disambiguation

For Latin-script text, tokenizes words and evaluates against a dual vocabulary: standard English words (nltk.corpus.words + telecom domain terminology) vs. an extensive dictionary of 80+ high-frequency Romanized Banglish phonetic markers (e.g., "valo", "onek", "kete", "nei", "hobe", "khub").

Supported Output Classifications & Telemetry Metadata:
Bangla Native Script
Romanized Banglish Phonetic Latin
English Standard Latin
Symbolic Emoticon Only

Additionally flags is_code_mixed = True when reviews combine native Bengali script and Latin vocabulary within the same sentence, enabling downstream logging and telemetry.

// Strategic Findings

Four Core Business Discoveries

1

Software Stability Outweighs Network Strength

App Login and software failures drove 77.1% of major monthly satisfaction drops (27 of 35 sharp swings from 2020 to 2026) and accounted for roughly 2.4× more negative reviews across the market (28,956 vs 12,083) than cellular network speed complaints. When an application update crashes on launch, subscribers immediately voice severe frustration regardless of their cellular signal.

2

Review Surges Mask Chronic Dissatisfaction

Promotional marketing campaigns flood app stores with short, superficial praise (accompanying 45.7% — 16 of 35 — of all major monthly rating swings). These volume waves artificially lift overall customer approval, temporarily masking recurring billing and account management grievances that resurface once promotional incentives end.

3

Billing Deductions Generate Universal Dissatisfaction

Roughly 9 out of 10 customer reviews concerning balance deductions and unexpected value-added charges express severe dissatisfaction (ranging from 89.8% to 92.9% negative across providers). Unexplained balance reductions destroy customer goodwill faster than any other operational issue.

4

Abrupt Interface Changes Trigger Immediate User Churn

Unannounced user interface overhauls and authentication resets consistently cause steep double-digit declines in customer satisfaction. This was prominently demonstrated when Banglalink released version 11.8.0 in April 2024, triggering a -11.2 percentage point monthly satisfaction drop driven by launch freezes, before hotfix releases (version 11.9.1) drove a +32.8 point rebound.

// Cross-Industry Strategic Value

Cross-Industry Executive Implications

Core management takeaways applicable to digital banks, mobile fintech wallets, and consumer subscription apps:

Staged Rollouts & Crash Monitoring

The dramatic customer backlash observed during Banglalink version 11.8.0 demonstrates that major application overhauls must be deployed through graduated phased rollouts with rapid hotfix rollouts before reaching all users.

Transparent Billing Audits

With billing inquiries driving 90%+ customer dissatisfaction across all market participants, mobile financial and subscription applications should offer an instant "Where Did My Balance Go?" transaction breakdown to prevent balance confusion.

Promotional Campaign Segmentation

Promotional reward campaigns trigger sudden surges in review volume (+50% or more in a month), accompanying 45.7% of major monthly swings. Executive leaders must separate promotional praise from steady operational feedback to avoid distorted customer experience metrics.
