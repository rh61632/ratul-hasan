About the Event

On April 28, 2025, the IEEE JUST Student Branch organized “Elevate Your Career: A Comprehensive Seminar on Freelancing” in collaboration with SR Dream IT. Held from 2:00 PM to 4:45 PM at the Central Gallery auditorium of Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), it was the branch’s biggest seminar to date, filling the auditorium to maximum capacity.

“We dreamed it, we planned it, and we made it happen! The IEEE JUST Student Branch successfully organized the biggest seminar to date in collaboration with SR Dream It. It was a truly mesmerizing experience — a result of the tireless efforts and dedication of our entire team.”

The seminar was designed to give university students direct exposure to freelancing pathways, digital marketing strategies, personal branding, and AI-driven growth in 2025.

Event Anchoring

Md. Ratul Hasan was the anchor for the program along with co-anchor Jannatun Nayeem. Together, they moderated the event from the opening announcements through the guest speaker sessions, audience interaction, the MoU signing ceremony, and the closing felicitations.

Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) Signing

During the seminar on April 28, 2025, an official Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed between the IEEE JUST Student Branch and SR Dream IT to establish ongoing collaboration in student skill development, freelancing, artificial intelligence, personal branding, and career readiness.
