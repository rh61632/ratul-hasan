The Seminar Vision & Historic MoU Signing

Bridging undergraduate university education with practical industry expectations is one of the most vital imperatives for engineering students. The “Elevate Your Career” seminar was organized by the IEEE JUST Student Branch in close collaboration with SR Dream IT to provide students with actionable career roadmaps, real-world skill development guidance, and mentorship from practicing technology professionals.

A crowning highlight of the event was the formal signing of an official Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the IEEE JUST Student Branch and SR Dream IT. This landmark agreement cemented an ongoing institutional alliance designed to bring workshops, specialized training bootcamps, and career placement resources directly to JUST students.
